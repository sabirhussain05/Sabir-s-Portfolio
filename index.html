About

Most businesses don't have a marketing problem. They have a systems problem.

Leads fall through the cracks. Follow-up depends on memory. Reporting lives in someone's head. Revenue plateaus — not because the offer is weak, but because the operational backend can't carry more volume.

That's the problem I solve. I design and build complete business operating systems: the CRM architecture, sales pipelines, automation layers, and AI workflows that connect marketing, sales, and operations into one machine.

I think like an operator, not a technician. Before anything gets built, I map how revenue actually moves through your business — then I remove the bottlenecks, automate the manual work, and build processes your team will actually use.

How I'm different

  • I solve bottlenecks that directly impact revenue
  • I connect systems instead of stacking disconnected tools
  • I build processes teams adopt — not shelfware
  • Every build is documented with SOPs and handover
